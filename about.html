Our Story

NCP Chemicals & Formulas – NCP-Chemicals

Founded over a decade ago in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, NCP Chemicals & Formulas was built on a simple belief — every Indian home deserves access to premium-quality, science-backed cleaning products at an affordable price.

Under our NCP-Chemicals, we manufacture a comprehensive range of household cleaning solutions — from gentle hand washes enriched with natural extracts to powerful detergents and fabric conditioners. Every product carries our promise: Clean, Safe, Pure.

Located at 2/66, Kulamaniannadam Main Road, Kalankeras Road, Madurai – 625017, we serve thousands of households and businesses across Tamil Nadu, delivering products that combine modern chemical science with nature's best ingredients.

Our Values & Mission

Safety First
All our products are formulated to be safe for your family — gentle on skin, tough on germs. Every formula is tested for efficacy and safety before reaching your home.
Scientific Innovation
We invest in research and development to continuously improve our formulas using the latest advances in cleaning science and natural ingredient technology.
Customer Trust
10+ years of consistent quality has earned us the trust of thousands of families. We never compromise on quality — because your home deserves the best.
Natural Ingredients
From aloe vera and apple extracts to lime, rose, honey and essential oils — we blend nature's goodness with science for superior results.
Eco-Conscious
Our biodegradable formulas are designed to minimize environmental impact while delivering maximum cleaning performance. A cleaner home, a cleaner planet.
Affordable Quality
Premium quality shouldn't break the bank. We price our products to be accessible to every Indian household — from small sachets to bulk 5-litre packs.
